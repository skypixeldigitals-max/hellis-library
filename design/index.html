Pick a direction

All three are clickable prototypes running on the same features and your real books. Try these in each one:

  • Type in the search box: fault, emily henry or dune
  • Tap Scan twice to see a book you own, then a book on your wishlist
  • Tap a cover to change read status, stars, or lend it to someone
  • Switch tabs; on Wishlist tap Got it and watch the series gap fill in
